Lawful data, by design

Every dataset and request passes the same review.

Our principles

Our six core principles

Known, lawful sources

We use only sources we are entitled to use: open data licences, direct agreements, consented panels and public sources whose terms permit collection.

Purpose limitation

Data is licensed for a stated purpose, which is reviewed before every order and recorded in the licence.

Data minimisation

We deliver only the fields required. Personal data is aggregated, pseudonymised or removed unless a valid legal basis covers its use.

Lineage & audit trail

For every delivery, we record the source, collection date, transformations and approvals.

Secure handling

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and download links are time-limited.

Rights respected

We honour opt-out and deletion requests and communicate them to buyers wherever required by law.

Review process

Our review criteria

Our compliance review is completed before any quotation is issued, at no cost to you.

Requester & purpose

The identity of the requester, the purpose of the request and whether that use is lawful in the relevant jurisdiction.

Source rights

Licences, terms of service, robots directives and any contracts governing the source.

Personal data & legal basis

Whether personal data is involved, the legal basis for processing and whether a DPIA is required.

Cross-border transfer

Transfer mechanisms such as SCCs or adequacy decisions, and any data localisation requirements.

Licence & contract

Scope of use, redistribution rights, retention period and whether a DPA or NDA is required.

Prohibited data

Data we do not provide or accept

We decline these requests, regardless of value.

  • Leaked, breached, hacked or stolen data
  • Personal contact lists (emails, phone numbers) without documented consent
  • Sensitive data about identifiable people: health, biometrics, religion, sexual orientation, political views, financial accounts
  • Data about children
  • Precise location histories of individuals
  • Data collected in breach of a website's terms or access controls
  • Data meant for surveillance, stalking, discrimination or other unlawful purposes
  • Government IDs, passwords or credentials
Shared responsibility

Buyer obligations

Every licence includes these commitments.

Use as licensed

Use the data solely for the purpose and scope defined in your licence.

No re-identification

Do not attempt to identify individuals from aggregated or de-identified data.

Keep it secure

Store the data securely and restrict access to authorised personnel.

Honour requests

Act promptly on any deletion or opt-out notices we communicate to you.
