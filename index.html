FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is API usage priced?

$0.001–$0.015 per request, up to 100 records each. First 100 requests a month are free, with 15% off above 100K and 30% off above 1M.

When should I buy a full licence?

When you need most of a dataset. The pricing calculator shows the break-even point.

What data can I request?

Business, market, geospatial, economic and AI training data. Quotes are free.

What data do you not provide?

Leaked data, contact lists without consent, sensitive personal data or data about children. See the full list.

How long does a custom request take?

Scope within 48 hours. Delivery in one to three weeks.

How do I become a data partner?

Apply through the Data Partner Program. Once verified, your data is listed and you are paid monthly.

Do you sign NDAs and DPAs?

Yes, including standard contractual clauses for cross-border transfers.
