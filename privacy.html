How we use it

Legal basis

We process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, for our legitimate interest in running a compliant business, and to comply with the law.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the service, such as hosting, form processing and email, and when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep request records for as long as we need them to provide the service and meet our legal and audit obligations, and then delete them.
